# Objects, Props, and Vehicles

Use for tools, weapons, furniture, gadgets, vehicles, machinery, and other designed objects.

## Plan first
1. Identify function, scale, viewpoint, materials, and any required distinguishing features.
2. Choose a consistent view: front, side, top, three-quarter, or orthographic.
3. Block in the primary silhouette and major volumes before seams, controls, or decoration.
4. Establish a light direction and a restrained material palette.
5. Layer components in physical order, keeping occlusion consistent.

## Shape and construction
- Make the object's purpose legible from its silhouette.
- Keep repeated components consistent in size and spacing unless variation is intentional.
- Align panels, wheels, handles, openings, and attachments to the same perspective.
- Use highlights and shadows to explain form, not as random decoration.
- Show only the details needed for the requested use; a technical diagram may need labels, while a concept illustration may not.

## Verification
Check silhouette, attachment points, repeated spacing, perspective consistency, layer order, required features, and whether the object reads clearly at the intended size.
