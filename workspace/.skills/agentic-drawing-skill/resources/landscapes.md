# Landscapes and Environments

Use for natural scenery, cityscapes, room interiors, establishing shots, and backgrounds.

## Plan first
1. Decide the scene's purpose, viewpoint, time/weather, mood, and aspect ratio.
2. Arrange foreground, middle ground, and background as large readable masses.
3. Establish a focal point and a clear path for the viewer's eye.
4. Choose a palette and light direction before adding small details.
5. Add detail in descending order of importance; keep low-priority regions quieter.

## Depth and composition
- Use overlap, relative scale, value contrast, and atmospheric softening to suggest depth.
- Avoid placing every high-contrast feature at the same visual priority.
- Keep horizon and major perspective lines consistent.
- Use foreground framing sparingly; it should lead attention, not obstruct the subject.
- For interiors, keep walls, floor, furniture, doors, and windows consistent with the chosen viewpoint.
- For stylized SVG scenes, use layered shapes and simplified value groups instead of trying to imitate every texture.

## Verification
Check focal-point clarity, depth separation, light consistency, unwanted tangencies, edge clipping, and whether the scene communicates the intended mood and setting.
