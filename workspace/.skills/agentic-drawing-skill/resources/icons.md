# Icons, Symbols, Badges, and Small Graphics

Use for app icons, UI symbols, simple logos, badges, and small reusable assets.

## Plan first
1. Identify where the asset will appear, its pixel size, background, and whether it needs to work in monochrome.
2. Reduce the concept to one recognizable silhouette or metaphor.
3. Use a consistent stroke width, corner treatment, and level of detail.
4. Prefer a small palette; ensure sufficient contrast against the intended background.
5. Design within a square or other specified viewBox with safe margins.

## Small-size rules
- Test at the actual target size; details that look good enlarged may disappear.
- Avoid thin gaps, tiny text, and near-touching shapes.
- Keep the visual center balanced, not merely the mathematical center.
- Use a filled silhouette or a few strong strokes rather than many small components.
- For a set of icons, keep scale, stroke width, perspective, and detail density consistent.

## Verification
Check readability at target size, contrast, padding, clipping, monochrome fallback if needed, and consistency with sibling assets. Do not add text unless requested or essential.
